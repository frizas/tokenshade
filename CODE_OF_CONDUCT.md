# Code of Conduct

TokenShade is a small project with a simple standard: be useful and do not make
other people pay the social cost of your participation.

We expect respectful, specific, good-faith collaboration. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, and deliberate
disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate this standard
and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do
not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.

Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, repair, and community
safety.
